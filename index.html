Inspection Calculator
Flat $100 fee, paid upon successful scheduling, for each inspection with a contract price of at least $600. If that inspection's own revenue reaches $2,000 or more, Contractor also earns an additional bonus of 10% of that inspection's total revenue — on top of the $100 flat fee, not instead of it. Add one row per inspection — amounts are rarely identical, so each is entered and calculated on its own.
